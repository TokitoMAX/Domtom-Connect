    01 · Local context

    Starting a business in Kenya, what to know

    Kenya is East Africa's main business and tech hub, with Nairobi at its heart and mobile money as part of everyday life.

    Business names and companies are registered with the Business Registration Service (BRS), largely online through eCitizen. Tax registration and county or sector licences follow. A local accountant will help you choose the right structure.

    02 · The method

    Six steps to start your business

    1. Clarify your project and market

    Who your customers are, what they will pay, how they pay, who your competitors are. This is the step most often rushed.

    2. Choose your legal structure

    Sole proprietorship, business name or limited company, depending on your project, partners and liability. Get advice from a local accountant or attorney.

    3. Register your business

    With the Business Registration Service (BRS), accessible online through the eCitizen platform. Check the current requirements and fees directly with the official body.

    4. Set up payments

    A business bank account, plus the payment methods your customers already use. Keep business and personal money separate from day one.

    5. Keep simple books

    Invoicing, tracking income and expenses, and weekly cash flow.

    6. Find your first customers

    Test your offer with real customers before investing heavily, including the diaspora if your product fits.
