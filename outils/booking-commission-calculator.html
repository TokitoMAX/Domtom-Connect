01 · How it works

What the figures mean

Booking platforms bring visibility, but they take a commission on every booking, including returning guests and people who would have found you anyway. Most owners never add up what it costs over a full year.

This calculator shows the yearly cost of commissions and how much you could keep by moving part of your bookings to your own website. The goal is not to leave platforms overnight, but to rebalance.
