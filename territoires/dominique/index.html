01 · Local context

Doing business in Dominica

Dominica, the Nature Island, sits between Guadeloupe and Martinique, with Roseau as its capital. Its rainforest, rivers and hot springs attract travellers looking for eco tourism and adventure rather than mass tourism.

After Hurricane Maria in 2017, many businesses rebuilt with resilience in mind. Being visible online, telling a strong story and taking direct bookings are key for eco lodges, guides and local producers.
