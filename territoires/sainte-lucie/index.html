01 · Local context

Doing business in Saint Lucia

Saint Lucia, with its capital Castries and the famous Pitons, is one of the best known destinations in the Eastern Caribbean. Tourism drives much of the economy, from hotels and villas to tour operators, restaurants and transport.

Its Kwéyòl, a French based creole, and its proximity to Martinique make it a natural bridge with the French islands. For many local businesses, the challenge is to win more direct bookings and customers instead of depending on platforms and agents.

02 · The challenges

What businesses face in Saint Lucia

Dependence on platforms

Many bookings go through platforms and agents that take a commission on every guest.

Seasonality

High season fills everything, low season is a worry. Planning cash flow and operations ahead makes the difference.

Being found before arrival

Visitors plan from abroad. If they cannot find and book you online, they choose someone else.
