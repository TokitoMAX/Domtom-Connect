04 · FAQ

Questions business owners ask us

Do you work with businesses outside the French islands?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses in St Lucia, Grenada, Sint Maarten, Dominica, Trinidad and Tobago, Jamaica and across the Caribbean, as well as in Africa and the diaspora. Calls happen on video, phone or WhatsApp.

Do you speak English?

Yes. Calls can be held in English or French, and everything we agree on is confirmed in writing by email or WhatsApp, so nothing gets lost.

How much does it cost?

The first 30 minute call is free. A one hour strategy session costs 100 euros and comes with a written action plan, deducted from the quote if you continue. Websites and ongoing support are quoted after the free call.

What is DTC Pulse?

DTC Pulse is the Domtom Connect business network app, connecting entrepreneurs and communities across the Caribbean, Africa and the diaspora. Each business creates and manages its own account.
