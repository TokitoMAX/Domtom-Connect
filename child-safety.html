Child Safety Standards & Protection des Enfants

Official Child Safety Standards, Community Guidelines & Anti-CSAE Policy for DTC Pulse, developed and operated by Domtom Connect.

ZERO TOLERANCE: Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE / CSAM)

Domtom Connect and the DTC Pulse application maintain a strict, zero-tolerance policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any violation results in immediate permanent account termination and immediate reporting to law enforcement authorities (including NCMEC and PHAROS).

Strictly Prohibited Content

Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

In compliance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy, Domtom Connect strictly and unequivocally prohibits the following content and behavior on DTC Pulse (package: com.domtomconnect.app):

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any photographic, video, illustrative, text-based, or AI-generated depiction of child sexual abuse, sexual acts, or exploitation involving individuals under 18 years of age.
  • Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE): Any solicitation, sexual extortion (sextortion), predatory grooming, or attempted contact with minors for illicit, abusive, or sexual purposes.
  • Sexualization of Minors: Any content that sexualizes minors, depicts children in an inappropriate or suggestive manner, or promotes harmful child exploitation.
  • Facilitation, Sharing or Trafficking: Any distribution, sharing, transmission, link-sharing, or commercialization of CSAE/CSAM materials.
In-App Reporting Mechanism & Safety Workflow

How DTC Pulse Protects Children & Responds to Reports

DTC Pulse incorporates built-in mechanisms to allow immediate user feedback, reporting, and law enforcement escalation:

1

In-App Reporting

Every post, story, comment, message, and profile features an easily accessible "Report / Signaler" button allowing users to flag suspect content in one tap.

2

Immediate Review (< 24h)

Child safety reports are prioritized at the highest level. Our moderation team reviews flagged items within 24 hours (usually in under 2 hours).

3

Instant Ban & Removal

Content violating CSAE standards is deleted immediately. Offending accounts are permanently banned and blocked across the platform.

4

Law Enforcement Reporting

We systematically report confirmed CSAE incidents to international bodies: NCMEC (via CyberTipline) and national authorities (PHAROS).

Interdictions absolues

Contenus et comportements strictement interdits

L'application DTC Pulse (identifiant : com.domtomconnect.app), éditée par Domtom Connect, interdit formellement et sans exception les actes suivants :

  • Pédopornographie et CSAM (Child Sexual Abuse Material) : Toute image, vidéo, illustration ou représentation générée par IA montrant des abus sexuels sur des mineurs de moins de 18 ans.
  • Sollicitation et prédation (Grooming) : Toute tentative de mise en contact, harcèlement ou manipulation d'un mineur à des fins sexuelles ou d'exploitation (CSAE).
  • Sexualisation de mineurs : Toute publication valorisant ou mettant en scène des mineurs de façon suggestive, pornographique ou dégradante.
  • Diffusion et trafic : Tout partage de lien, facilitation, diffusion ou stockage de contenus illicites relatifs aux mineurs.
